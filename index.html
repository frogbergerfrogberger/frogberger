About

Making sense of the world around us

FROGBERGER is the working name of UK-based visual artist Leo Sparks-Wilson. Working across as many mediums as he can get his hands on, FROGBERGER projects aim to explore new and unusual representations of the self and the creatures we are.

With key focuses on identity, symbolism, bodily distortion and emotional disconnect, FROGBERGER uses portraiture, surrealism and a unique visual language to explore the human condition. FROGBERGER's work is often unsettling, but always with a sense of humour and a desire to connect with others through shared experience.

Alongside independent studio work, FROGBERGER is available for collaborative projects, publication work and client-led visual design.
